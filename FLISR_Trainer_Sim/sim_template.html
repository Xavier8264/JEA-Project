  How the bench models the hardware
  

    

      
What runs. setup() and loop() are a line-for-line port of everything below
      “NOTHING BELOW HERE NEEDS EDITING”. Pins, colors, timing and the segment table are read out of the
      .ino at build time, not retyped. The build refuses to run if the sketch logic changes without the port being updated.

      
Time. millis() starts at 0 on reset. delay() in setup advances it, then
      loop() runs once per millisecond. Button contacts are timestamped, so a tap shorter than
      DEBOUNCE_MS is ignored, the same as on the board. The clock pauses while this tab is in the background.

      
Buttons are ideal contacts to GND, with no mechanical bounce. Hold one with the mouse, a finger, or Space/Enter when it has focus.
      Pressing two at once behaves as the sketch does: only the first one found in a pass is handled.

    

    

      
Pixels show the leds[] buffer as of the last FastLED.show(). Brightness, color
      correction and dithering are applied after that, on the wire, and are not drawn. The FastLED 3.10.5 power limiter is
      computed every frame and shown under Outputs.

      
Placement. Pixels are evenly spaced on the straight line between each run’s From and To node in the LED
      sheet. Run 1 starts SEG_SPLIT[0] pixels south of N1, per the note on run 1. Under-board runs are drawn offset
      0.45 in so they don’t cover the runs above them. Device LEDs sit 0.75 in right and 0.60 in up from their Element
      point, which is marked with a leader line.
