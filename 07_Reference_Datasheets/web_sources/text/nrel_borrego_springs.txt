Goal: Demonstrate the viability of a microgrid to manage high amounts
(up to 100%) of renewable, intermittent energy resources to meet
community load that can be replicated by others while leveraging (post-
project) off-the-shelf software
Impact: Successful implementation of the largest microgrid in North
America will prove that a community-scale, highly renewable
microgrid can be implemented with economic benefits.
